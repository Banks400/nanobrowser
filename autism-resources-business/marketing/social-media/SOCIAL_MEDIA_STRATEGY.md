## PLATFORM 1: PINTEREST

### Why Pinterest First
- Average pin has a 3–6 month "shelf life" (vs. 48 hours on Instagram)
- Autism parenting is one of the highest-searched topics in the parenting vertical
- Pinterest users actively search for solutions — highest buyer intent outside of Google
- Free traffic compounds over time; pins from year 1 still drive traffic in year 3
- No need to build an audience from scratch — people find your pins via search

### Account Setup
- **Account type:** Business account (free)
- **Profile name:** SpectrumReady | Autism Parent Resources
- **Bio:** "Helping autism parents navigate benefits, therapies, IEPs & more. Plain-English guides + free resources."
- **Profile link:** Your website URL
- **Enable Rich Pins:** Automatically pull metadata from your website (increases click-through rate)

### Board Structure (Create These Immediately)
1. Autism Benefits & Programs (SSI, Medicaid, ABLE)
2. IEP Tips & Special Education Rights
3. Autism Therapies & Providers
4. Autism Insurance Help
5. Newly Diagnosed Autism Families
6. Autism Transition Planning (Teens & Adults)
7. Autism Home Strategies & Daily Life
8. Autism Sensory Tips & Tools
9. Free Autism Parent Resources
10. Autism Organization & Planning (planners, binders)

### Pin Creation System (5–10 pins/day)

**Pin formats that convert best in this niche:**
- **List pins:** "7 Benefits Autism Families Are Missing" with numbered list visual
- **Quote/stat pins:** "1 in 36 children has autism — and most families miss out on these 5 benefits"
- **Before/after:** "Before: Frustrated at IEP meetings. After: Walking in with confidence."
- **Step-by-step:** "How to Appeal an Insurance Denial (Step 1 of 4)"
- **Infographic:** "Types of Autism Therapies at a Glance"

**Pin design specs:**
- Size: 1000 x 1500 px (2:3 ratio)
- Use Canva (free or Pro)
- Brand colors: Purple/indigo palette matching website
- Text overlay is critical — most users see pins without reading the description
- Use faces/people sparingly (can feel exploitative in disability niche)
- Clean, professional, not clinical

**Pin copy formula:**
- Title (bold, large): Hook/benefit statement
- Subtitle: One sentence elaboration
- CTA: "Save for later" or "Get the free guide"
- Your branding: Logo/URL in corner

**Pin description (for SEO):**
Use 3–5 relevant keyword phrases naturally in the description:
> "If your child has autism, these 7 government benefits could change your family's financial situation. From SSI to Medicaid waivers to ABLE accounts — our Autism Benefits Navigator covers every program, how to apply, and what to say. Free guide at SpectrumReady.com. #autismparent #autismresources #autismbenefits #IEP #autismmom"

### Pinterest Posting Schedule
- Post 5–10 pins/day (can repin others' content for 30–40% of volume)
- Your own content: 3–5 original pins/day minimum
- Use Tailwind (scheduling tool: $15/month) to automate and post at optimal times

## PLATFORM 2: FACEBOOK GROUPS

### Why Facebook Groups Are Critical for Early Revenue
- Massive existing communities of autism parents (hundreds of thousands of members)
- High-intent, emotionally engaged audience
- Immediate feedback loop on what resonates
- Direct path from post → lead magnet → email subscriber → customer
- No algorithm to fight — you're fishing where the fish are

### Target Groups to Join
Research and join these types of groups (search on Facebook):
- "Autism Parents Support Group" — 280,000+ members
- "Autism Speaks Community" groups
- "[Your State] Autism Parents"
- "ABA Therapy Parents"
- "IEP Help for Parents"
- "Autism Moms Support"
- "HCBS Medicaid Waiver Parents"
- "Special Needs Parenting Support"
- "Autism Parents — Early Intervention"
- Local autism parent groups in your city/state

**Join 10–15 groups.** Be strategic — larger, more active groups first.

### Facebook Group Strategy (4 Phases)

**Phase 1: Listening (Week 1–2)**
Before posting anything, spend 1 week just reading. What questions come up most? What pain points are people expressing? What information seems to be missing? This research is gold.

**Phase 2: Providing Value (Week 2–4)**
Answer questions in the group with genuine, detailed, helpful responses. Don't mention your products. Just be the knowledgeable, helpful person who gives great answers.

Post helpful content:
- "Here's how the SSI application works if your child has autism..."
- "IEP tip that most parents don't know..."
- "Question for parents: What's the one thing you wish you'd known right after diagnosis?"

**Phase 3: Soft Introduction (Week 4–6)**
When you've established credibility, you can mention the free guide:
> "I put together a free resource on the top 5 programs autism families should know about. If anyone wants it, I'm happy to share — just comment or DM me."

This soft approach gets 10–30 DMs per post in active groups. Each person who DMs gets the free guide and is added to your email list.

**Phase 4: Ongoing Presence**
- Post 2–3 times per week across all groups
- Always lead with value, not promotion
- Occasionally mention paid guides in context of solving specific problems
- Become known as "the benefits/IEP person" in each group

**What NOT to do in Facebook groups:**
✗ Post promotional content immediately after joining
✗ Spam multiple groups with identical posts (group admins will ban you)
✗ Post links without asking (many groups prohibit this — read the rules)
✗ Be dismissive or provide incorrect information (credibility is everything)

## PLATFORM 3: TIKTOK / INSTAGRAM REELS

### Content Strategy: "The Autism Parent Expert"

The fastest-growing autism parent content on TikTok falls into these categories:
- Little-known tips and facts that shock people
- "Things I wish I'd known" format
- IEP/school advocacy content (very high engagement)
- Benefits and financial information
- Therapy explanations in plain language

### Video Framework (Use This for 80% of Videos)

**The Hook + Reveal Formula:**
1. **Hook (first 2 seconds):** Shocking statement or question that creates pattern interrupt
2. **Problem:** "Most autism parents don't know..."
3. **Reveal/Tip:** The useful information
4. **CTA:** "Save this, follow for more, free guide in bio"

**Examples of proven hook formats:**

*"If your child has autism, stop scrolling. You need to know this."*

*"The government is required to give your child this — but they'll never tell you."*

*"Most autism families lose $X every year because of this one mistake."*

*"Here's what I wish someone told me the week my child was diagnosed."*

*"Your child's school legally has to provide this. Here's exactly how to ask for it."*

### Content Calendar (3–5 Videos/Week)

**Monday:** IEP/School tip
*"One thing to say at your next IEP meeting that changes everything"*

**Tuesday:** Benefits/financial content
*"3 benefits autism families are entitled to (most never claim)"*

**Wednesday:** Therapy/clinical content
*"The question you should ask every ABA provider"*

**Thursday:** Emotional/community content
*"If you're newly diagnosed, here's what I want you to hear"*

**Friday/Weekend:** Success stories, Q&A responses, or trending audio

### Video Production Tips (No Fancy Equipment Needed)
- Shoot vertically on your phone
- Good lighting: face a window or buy a $15 ring light
- Clean, simple background
- Use captions (85% of TikTok is watched without sound) — TikTok auto-generates these
- 30–60 seconds is ideal length; hooks should land in the first 2 seconds
- End with a clear CTA every time

### Hashtags (Use 3–5 per post, don't overstuff)
- #autismparent
- #autismmom
- #autismdad
- #autismadvocate
- #IEPhelp
- #autismbenefits
- #specialneedsparenting
- #autismresources
- #autismawareness (broad but high volume)
- #neurodivergent

### Link in Bio Setup
Use a linktree or Stan Store to create a simple link page:
- Free Guide Download (most prominent CTA)

## PAID ADVERTISING (Start Month 3+)

### Facebook / Instagram Ads

**Campaign #1: Lead Generation (Free Guide)**
- Objective: Lead generation
- Budget: $10–$20/day
- Target:
  - Interests: Autism, autism awareness, special education, IEP, Applied Behavior Analysis
  - Parents with children 1–18
  - Age 25–50
  - Exclude: people who have already subscribed to your email list
- Ad format: Single image or video
- Creative: Video works best (30-second version of your best TikTok content)
- CTA: "Get the Free Guide"
- Landing page: Your opt-in page

Expected results: $3–$8 cost per lead (email subscriber)

**Campaign #2: Retargeting (Warm Audience)**
- Objective: Conversions
- Budget: $10/day
- Target:
  - Website visitors in last 30 days
  - Email list custom audience
  - Video viewers (people who watched 50%+ of your video ads)
- Ad creative: Testimonial-based ("What one mom said after the IEP meeting")
- CTA: Shop now / Learn more
- Landing page: Bundle product page

Expected results: 3–5x ROAS (return on ad spend) on warm audiences

**Campaign #3: Lookalike Audience (Scale)**
- Once you have 500+ email subscribers, create a lookalike audience from your customer list
- This finds new people who "look like" your best customers
- Budget: $30–$50/day once validated

### Pinterest Ads

- Amplify your top-performing organic pins
- Budget: $5–$10/day
- Promote pins linking to your free guide opt-in page
- Expected: $2–$5 CPM (lower than Meta), good CTR from high-intent audience

## INFLUENCER / PARTNERSHIP STRATEGY (Month 4+)

### Autism Bloggers and Content Creators
- Find bloggers in the autism parent space with 5,000–50,000 readers
- Offer: 30% affiliate commission on all sales they drive
- Provide them: Free copy of guides, custom affiliate link, pre-written social copy
- Tools: Gumroad or ThriveCart built-in affiliate tracking

**Where to find them:**
- Search Pinterest for autism parent bloggers (see who's getting lots of repins)
- Search Google: "autism parent blog"
- Instagram search: #autismmom (look for creators with 5k–100k followers)
- Facebook group admins with large groups

### Therapy Provider Partnerships
- ABA clinics, speech therapy practices, OT clinics
- Offer: Bulk licensing of guides ($XXX for unlimited use with families) OR affiliate commission
- They benefit: Giving families resources makes them better clients
- They win: Reduced admin time explaining programs; you win: distribution

### Autism Organization Partnerships
- Contact local autism organizations about carrying your guides in their resource library
- Offer complimentary copies for their staff + affiliate program

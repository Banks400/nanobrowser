### NURTURE EMAIL B (Week 3)

**Subject:** "I wish someone had told me this when my child was first diagnosed"

Hi [FIRST NAME],

One of the most common things I hear from autism parents is: "I wish someone had told me this earlier."

Today's "wish I'd known" tip:

**The Extended School Year (ESY) is not the same as summer school — and most families never get it.**

ESY is the continuation of your child's IEP services during summer and school breaks. Not remedial classes — their actual therapy services (speech, ABA, OT).

The key: You have to prove your child regresses during breaks. The way to do that is to keep data.

Before summer, write down your child's current skill levels (words they can say, tasks they can complete, behaviors). After summer, compare. If they've lost ground, you have documentation for an ESY request.

Schools will almost never volunteer this information. You have to ask — and show up with data.

This is one of dozens of IEP provisions covered in our **IEP Mastery guide**, including templates for making requests, scripts for difficult conversations, and the escalation process for when the school says no.

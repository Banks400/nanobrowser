## STEP 6: ETSY SEO — HOW THE ALGORITHM WORKS

Etsy search ranks listings based on:
1. **Title relevance** — keywords in the listing title
2. **Tag relevance** — all 13 tags used, specific phrases
3. **Listing quality score** — click-through rate, conversion rate, reviews
4. **Recency** — new listings get a temporary boost
5. **Customer & market experience score** — your shop reputation

### Keyword Research Tips
- Search your own keywords on Etsy and look at autocomplete suggestions
- Use EtsyHunt.com (free) or Sale Samurai to see search volumes
- Focus on 3–4 word phrases (long-tail) — less competition than single words
- Repeat your most important keywords across title, tags, AND first 40 words of description

### Top Keywords to Own on Etsy
Based on autism parent search behavior:
- "autism parent guide" — moderate competition, high intent
- "IEP guide for parents" — lower competition, very high intent
- "autism benefits guide" — low competition, high value purchase
- "autism insurance appeal" — very low competition, desperate buyer
- "autism transition guide" — low competition

## STEP 7: GETTING YOUR FIRST 5 REVIEWS (Critical)

Etsy listings with zero reviews convert poorly. Your first priority is getting 5 reviews.

**Strategy:**
1. Share your Etsy listings in 3–5 autism parent Facebook groups with a soft intro post
2. Ask 5 people you know (family, friends who have children with autism) to purchase and review honestly
3. Send a follow-up message to every buyer: *"Thank you so much for your purchase. If the guide helped you, a review on Etsy helps other families find it. No pressure — I just want as many families as possible to have this information."*
4. Respond to every review (it signals to Etsy that you're an engaged seller)

**First review milestone:** After 5 reviews, your listing quality score improves significantly. After 10, you're eligible for Etsy Ads which can accelerate organic ranking.

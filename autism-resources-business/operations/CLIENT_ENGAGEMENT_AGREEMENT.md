## 1. NATURE OF SERVICES

SpectrumReady provides **educational information, advocacy guidance, and navigation support** to families of individuals with autism spectrum disorder. Specifically, the services described in this agreement consist of:

- Providing general educational information about government benefit programs, insurance rights, special education law, and related topics
- Reviewing documents (IEPs, denial letters, evaluation reports, applications) and offering guidance on content and strategy
- Helping clients prepare for meetings, applications, and appeal processes
- Providing templates, worksheets, and reference materials for client use

**These services do not constitute, and shall not be construed as:**

- Legal advice or legal representation of any kind
- Medical advice or medical recommendations specific to any individual
- Financial advice, tax advice, or financial planning services
- Social work services subject to professional licensing requirements

SpectrumReady is not a law firm. [Advisor Name] is not an attorney, physician, certified financial planner, or licensed social worker. The engagement described in this agreement does not create an attorney-client, physician-patient, or any other licensed professional relationship.

## 2. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

Client agrees to:

a) Provide accurate and complete information relevant to the services requested

b) Understand that the guidance provided is educational in nature and based on general knowledge of how programs and systems work — not a determination of whether the specific client or their family member qualifies for any specific benefit or program

c) Consult a licensed professional (attorney, physician, financial advisor) for decisions that require licensed professional judgment, including but not limited to:
   - Due process hearings and formal legal proceedings
   - Estate planning, guardianship, and Special Needs Trust drafting
   - Specific medical treatment decisions
   - Tax planning and preparation

d) Verify all benefit amounts, income thresholds, and program rules directly with the relevant agency before taking action, as these change regularly and information provided may not reflect current figures

e) Maintain confidentiality of any materials, templates, or proprietary content provided by SpectrumReady

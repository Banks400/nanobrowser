## SECTION 2: STAN STORE SETUP (PRIMARY STOREFRONT)

### Step-by-Step Setup

**Step 1: Create your account**
- Go to stan.store/signup
- Choose "Creator" plan ($29/month)
- Connect your Stripe account for payments
- Set up direct deposit

**Step 2: Upload your products**
For each PDF product:
1. Click "Add Product" → "Digital Download"
2. Upload your PDF file (max 250MB)
3. Set the price
4. Write the product description (copy from your website's product cards)
5. Upload product thumbnail image (create in Canva, 1:1 ratio)
6. Enable "Thank you page" and customize the post-purchase message

**Step 3: Create your bundle**
1. Add Product → "Bundle"
2. Select all 6 individual products
3. Set bundle price ($127)
4. Write bundle description (copy from website)

**Step 4: Set up your storefront page**
This becomes your link-in-bio destination:
- Profile photo (your logo)
- Short bio (copy from website)
- Feature the bundle at the top
- Then individual guides
- Then free guide opt-in (Stan Store has a built-in email capture)

**Step 5: Set up order bumps (upsells)**
When someone buys an individual guide, offer the bundle upgrade:
- Order bump: "Add the Complete Toolkit for just $80 more" (vs. $127 standalone)
- 15–25% of buyers will take this offer

## SECTION 3: PDF DESIGN PROCESS

### Canva Design Workflow

**For each guide:**
1. Open Canva and search for "eBook" templates
2. Choose a template that matches your brand (purple/indigo palette)
3. Create a title page with:
   - Guide title (bold, large)
   - Subtitle
   - Your brand name and logo
   - "spectrumready.com"
4. Create a table of contents page
5. Design body pages using a consistent layout:
   - Clear H1, H2, H3 hierarchy
   - Colored accent boxes for key tips/callouts
   - Checklists with checkbox styling
   - Tables where data comparisons are needed
   - Footer on every page with brand name + URL
6. Create a back cover with:
   - Your brand logo
   - Links to website, email
   - Copyright notice
   - Disclaimer (important — see legal section)

**Design consistency across all guides:**
- Same font family (Inter or Nunito recommended)
- Same color palette (purple primary, amber accent, white backgrounds)
- Same header/footer design
- Same callout box styles
- Each guide has a unique accent color to differentiate

**Export settings:**
- Export as PDF (standard or PDF print quality)
- Enable "Compress file" to keep download size manageable
- Target file size: 5–15 MB per guide

## SECTION 4: FULFILLMENT SOP

### Automated Fulfillment (Goal: 0 manual steps)

With Stan Store or ThriveCart + BookFunnel:

1. Customer completes purchase on Stan Store
2. Payment processes via Stripe automatically
3. Stan Store sends customer an automatic "Your order is ready" email with download link
4. Customer clicks link, downloads PDF immediately
5. Stan Store triggers your email platform to add the customer to your "customers" email list segment
6. Post-purchase email sequence starts automatically

**You do nothing for the standard fulfillment flow.** It runs 24/7/365 without you.

### When Something Goes Wrong (Troubleshooting SOP)

**Customer says they didn't receive the download:**
1. Check if their order is in your Stan Store dashboard
2. Check if their email is correct (typo is the most common cause)
3. Click "Resend download" in Stan Store dashboard
4. If still not received, manually email them the PDF as an attachment

**Customer says the PDF won't open:**
1. Ensure they have a PDF reader (Adobe Acrobat, Preview on Mac, any browser)
2. Ask them to try opening in a different browser
3. As last resort, provide a Google Drive link to the file

**Customer requests a refund:**
1. Issue immediately in Stripe/Stan Store without asking questions
2. Send a personal email: "Refund issued — I'm sorry it wasn't the right fit. If there's anything specific that could have made it better, I'd love to know."
3. This approach prevents chargebacks (which are much worse than refunds) and occasionally recovers the sale

## SECTION 5: CUSTOMER SERVICE SOP

### Response Time Standards
- Email queries: Within 24 hours (M–F), within 48 hours (weekends)
- Facebook messages: Within 12 hours (high priority — these are active buyers)
- Instagram DMs: Within 24 hours
- Comments: Respond within 4 hours when possible

### Customer Service Templates

**"I can't download the file"**
> Hi [Name], I'm sorry you're having trouble! Let me help. Can you try opening the link in a different browser (Chrome usually works best)? If that doesn't work, reply here and I'll send you the guide directly. Thank you for your patience!

**"I accidentally bought the wrong guide"**
> Hi [Name], No problem at all! I've issued a refund for the [incorrect guide]. If you'd like the [correct guide], here's the direct link: [link]. Please let me know if there's anything else I can help with!

**"I want a refund"**
> Hi [Name], I've processed your refund and it should be back in your account within 3–5 business days. I'm sorry the guide wasn't what you were looking for! If you don't mind sharing what we could have done better, I'd really appreciate the feedback.

**"I have a question about my child's specific situation"**
> Hi [Name], Thank you for reaching out! While I'm not able to provide specific legal or medical advice for individual situations, the [relevant guide] covers [specific topic] in detail on pages X–Y. For your specific situation, I'd also recommend consulting with [a special education attorney / a benefits counselor / your child's treatment team]. I hope this helps!

## SECTION 6: LEGAL REQUIREMENTS

### Essential Pages to Add to Website

**1. Privacy Policy** (legally required)
Must include:
- What data you collect (name, email, purchase info)
- How you use it (email marketing, order fulfillment)
- Third parties you share with (Stripe, email platform)
- Cookie policy
- User rights (access, deletion)
- Contact information

*Use a privacy policy generator: termly.io or privacypolicygenerator.info (customize for your business)*

**2. Terms of Service**
Must include:
- What you're selling (digital products, personal use only)
- No refund exceptions (or your guarantee terms)
- Prohibited uses (no resale, no redistribution)
- Intellectual property notice
- Limitation of liability

**3. Earnings/Results Disclaimer**
Especially important for any content that could be construed as financial advice:
> "The information in this guide is for educational purposes only and does not constitute legal, financial, or medical advice. Every family's situation is unique. Please consult qualified professionals (attorneys, financial advisors, medical professionals) for advice specific to your situation."

**4. Affiliate Disclosure** (if you use affiliate links anywhere)
> "This page may contain affiliate links. If you make a purchase through these links, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you."

### Business Structure
- Form an LLC in your home state (protects personal assets, tax flexibility)
- Cost: $50–$500 depending on state
- File with your Secretary of State's website
- Get an EIN (Employer Identification Number) from IRS.gov (free)
- Open a separate business checking account
- Consider consulting a CPA for tax structure advice

### Copyright Protection
- Every PDF should include on the first or last page:
  > "© [Year] SpectrumReady. All rights reserved. This guide is for personal use only. No part of this publication may be reproduced, distributed, or transmitted in any form without prior written permission from the publisher."
- Register your copyright at copyright.gov ($65/filing) for important works

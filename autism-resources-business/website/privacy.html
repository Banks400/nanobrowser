Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

SpectrumReady ("we," "us," or "our") operates spectrumready.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or make a purchase.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Name and email address (when you sign up for our email list or free guide)
  • Payment information (processed securely by Stripe — we do not store card numbers)
  • Purchase history and download activity
  • Communications you send us

We also automatically collect:

  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Pages visited and time spent on site (via Google Analytics)
  • Referral source (how you found us)

How We Use Your Information

  • Fulfill purchases and deliver digital products
  • Send order confirmations and receipts
  • Send you the email communications you signed up for (you can unsubscribe anytime)
  • Improve our website and products
  • Respond to customer service inquiries
  • Comply with legal obligations

Email Communications

By providing your email address, you agree to receive our newsletter and product emails. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. We do not sell your email address to third parties.

Third Parties We Work With

  • Stripe: Payment processing (see stripe.com/privacy)
  • Stan Store / ConvertKit: Digital product delivery and email marketing
  • Google Analytics: Website analytics (anonymized)
  • Facebook: Advertising and analytics (if you've interacted with our Facebook Pixel)

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time
  • Request correction of inaccurate data

To exercise these rights, email us at: privacy@spectrumready.com

Cookies

We use cookies for analytics (Google Analytics) and advertising retargeting (Facebook Pixel). You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Children's Privacy

Our website is directed at adults (parents and caregivers). We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

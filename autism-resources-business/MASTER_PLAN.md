## MARKET OPPORTUNITY

### Why This Market Is Exceptional
- **1 in 36 children** in the US are diagnosed with autism (CDC 2024) — 3.5M+ autism families
- Parents describe feeling "completely lost" after diagnosis — overwhelmed by systems, jargon, and bureaucracy
- Free resources exist but are **fragmented, bureaucratic, and hard to navigate**
- Parents will pay premium for **organized, actionable, plain-English guidance**
- Emotional purchase trigger: protecting their child's future — among the highest-intent buyers on earth

### Market Size
- ~300,000 new autism diagnoses per year in the US alone
- Growing 15–20% annually
- Digital products market for special needs parents: underserved, low competition
- Etsy alone has 5,000+ autism products listed; top sellers earn $10k–$50k/month
- Neurodivergent planner PDFs: only 1,200 competing listings = low competition, high buyer intent

### Competitor Landscape
| Competitor Type | What They Do | Your Advantage |
|---|---|---|
| Autism Speaks, ASAN | Free toolkits, fragmented | Paid but organized, actionable, comprehensive |
| Teachers Pay Teachers sellers | Mostly classroom tools | You focus on PARENT advocacy & navigation |
| Individual bloggers | Blog posts, free content | Structured PDFs they can print and use |
| Etsy printable sellers | Visual charts, basic | Deep, comprehensive guides with real strategy |
| Book publishers | $25+ paperbacks | Instant download, updated, specific use cases |

**Gap in the market:** No one is selling a comprehensive "operating manual for autism parenting" — everything from diagnosis to adulthood in a structured, affordable, instantly downloadable format.

## MARKETING STRATEGY

### Organic (Months 1–4, Primary Focus)

**1. Pinterest (Highest ROI for this niche)**
- Create 5–10 pins per week linking to blog posts/product pages
- Target keywords: "autism parent resources," "IEP tips," "autism benefits guide"
- Pinterest users are 80% women, research-heavy, buying intent
- Estimated: 500–2,000 monthly visitors by month 3

**2. Facebook Groups (Fastest Early Sales)**
- Join 10–15 active autism parent Facebook groups
- Provide value for 2 weeks before any promotion
- Share free lead magnet → build list → convert to buyers
- Top groups: Autism Parents Support Group (280k members), Autism Speaks Community
- Estimated: 20–40 email subs per week

**3. TikTok/Instagram Reels (Fastest Audience Growth)**
- Short videos: "Did you know your child qualifies for X?" hook format
- "Things autism parents need to know" series
- Behind the scenes: "how I use this guide"
- Post 3–5x per week
- Estimated: 0–1,000 followers in 60 days; 5,000+ by month 4

**4. YouTube Shorts & Long-Form**
- "How to get your child's ABA therapy covered by insurance"
- "5 benefits autism families are missing out on"
- Long-form builds trust; shorts drives discovery
- Estimated: traffic boosts once 100+ subscribers

**5. SEO Content Blog**
- 1–2 blog posts per week targeting long-tail keywords
- Topics: "how to appeal insurance denial for ABA therapy," "what is a Medicaid waiver autism"
- 6–12 month payoff but compounds forever
- Target: 10,000 organic monthly visitors by month 12

### Paid (Month 3+, After Validating Organically)

**Facebook/Instagram Ads**
- Start budget: $10–$20/day on free lead magnet
- Target: Parents of children with autism, ages 28–50, interests: autism, special education, IEP
- Lookalike audience from email list once 500+ subscribers
- Expected CAC: $5–$15 per email subscriber; $25–$50 per customer

**Pinterest Ads**
- Amplify best-performing organic pins
- Lower CPCs than Meta, high buyer intent
- $5–$10/day test budget

### Referral & Affiliate (Month 4+)
- 30% affiliate commission for autism bloggers, therapists, advocates
- Partner with ABA therapy centers (offer bulk licensing)
- Guest posts on autism blogs with backlinks to products

## CONTENT STRATEGY & BRAND POSITIONING

### Brand Voice
- **Empowering, not pitying** — "You've got this" not "this is so hard"
- **Jargon-free, plain English** — speak like a knowledgeable friend
- **Hope + Action** — every piece of content ends with a next step
- **Credibility without credentials** — cite sources, be thorough, cite real programs

### Content Pillars
1. **Know Your Rights** — IEP rights, insurance mandates, IDEA law
2. **Follow the Money** — SSI, Medicaid, waivers, ABLE, grants
3. **Build Your Team** — finding therapists, evaluators, advocates
4. **Home Strategies** — what to do at home between therapy sessions
5. **Plan Ahead** — transition planning, adult services, housing

### Hooks That Convert (proven in this niche)
- "Most autism families miss out on $X in benefits they're entitled to"
- "Your child's school legally must provide this — most don't tell you"
- "If your child was diagnosed in the last 2 years, read this first"
- "The ONE call you need to make this week if your child has autism"
- "I wish someone had told me this when my child was first diagnosed"
